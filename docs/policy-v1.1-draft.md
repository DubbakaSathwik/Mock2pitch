## TERMS

## 1. About Mock2Pitch
Mock2Pitch is a practice tool for students. You upload a PDF of your slides, rehearse out loud in a private virtual jury room, and receive AI-generated feedback after your session.

## 2. Who can use it
You sign in with your Google account. You must give your college, year and branch before you start. You are responsible for the content you upload and the details you enter.

## 3. What you can upload
PDF slides of up to 10 slides and 5 MB. Do not upload anything you do not have the right to use. Do not upload confidential information, or personal information about other people.

## 4. Daily limit
You can start up to 5 practice sessions per day. Uploading a new deck and retaking a deck each count as one session. The day resets at midnight, India Standard Time. A deck you cancel before it is read still counts as a session.

## 5. AI feedback
Feedback, scripts and scores are generated by AI and may contain mistakes. Treat them as guidance for practice, not as an assessment or a guarantee of any result.

## 6. Acceptable use
Do not misuse the service, try to access other users' data, or try to disrupt it. The owner may pause or block accounts that do.

## 7. Availability
The service is provided as it is, and AI providers can be slow or unavailable at times. We do not promise uninterrupted service.

## 8. Changes to these terms
When the Terms or Privacy Policy change, you will be asked to accept the new version before you continue. A practice session you have already started can still be submitted.

## 9. Deleting your account
You can delete your account at any time from Settings. This removes your profile, decks, session data, reports and ratings.

## PRIVACY POLICY

## 1. What we collect
- From Google: your name, email address and profile photo.
- From you: your college, year and branch.
- Your uploads and inputs: the PDF you upload, an image of each slide, and the details you give about your presentation (audience, goal, short summary and time limit).
- From your practice: sessions, transcripts, scores, feedback and the star ratings you give.

## 2. How your slides and details are used
Your PDF, slide images and the details you enter are stored privately. They are sent to **Google's Gemini AI service** to read each slide and to suggest key points and a script. They are kept until you delete your deck or your account, so you can retake a deck without uploading it again.

## 3. Who can see your reports
Only you can see your reports. The owner sees aggregate usage, your profile details and the star ratings you give. The owner cannot see your slides, scripts, transcripts or feedback.

## 4. Your recordings
Audio recordings are sent to an **NVIDIA-hosted speech service** to create the transcript. They are deleted from our servers once your report is generated. Reports keep transcripts, scores and feedback, never audio.
If you play back your recording after submitting, that audio is held only in your own browser on your device. It is not uploaded and is lost when you close or leave the page.

## 5. AI providers
To provide the feedback we use these providers:
- Google (Gemini) to read your slides and write scripts.
- NVIDIA-hosted models to turn speech into text and to write your report from the transcript.
We send them only what is needed for that step. We do not use your content to train our own models. Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy policy; [check and confirm what each provider says about training on API data before you publish this sentence].

## 6. Where data is stored
Data is stored with our infrastructure providers for the database (Supabase) and file storage (Cloudflare). Data may be processed in countries other than your own.

## 7. Your choices
You can edit your name and profile in Settings, cancel an unfinished deck, and delete your account at any time. Deleting your account removes your files and data.

The service is meant for college students. [Add your minimum age if you have one.]

## How to publish this in the project

1. Run the app, sign in with the **owner** Google account at `/admin/sign-in`.
2. Open **Terms and Privacy** in the admin menu (`/admin/terms`).
3. On the **Terms** tab, replace the text with the TERMS section above. On the **Privacy Policy** tab, replace the text with the PRIVACY section.
4. Click **Publish**, accept the suggested label `v1.1`, add a note such as "Gemini + NVIDIA providers, playback, contact", and confirm.
5. Every student must accept the new version on their next sign-in. No code change or deploy is needed.
6. Check `/terms` and `/privacy` while signed out to see the live text.

Publish this **after** the app is live, or before it, either works. Do it before real students use the new Gemini flow.
